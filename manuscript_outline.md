## Methods

The graph model is a site-agnostic, HCAN-derived adaptation of Shao, Fu & Chen
(2023), *BMC Bioinformatics*, doi:10.1186/s12859-023-05241-2. It removes the
published architecture's site meta-path for LOSO and external-site evaluation;
see [the HCAN adaptation note](results/hcan_adaptation_notes.md) for the
protocol and comparability caveats.
