# Data and ethics statement

ABIDE I and ABIDE II are public, de-identified neuroimaging datasets assembled
by multi-site consortia under their applicable institutional-review-board (IRB)
oversight. This study analyzes those existing de-identified public data and
does not recruit participants or collect new data; new IRB review is typically
not required for secondary analysis of public de-identified data of this type.
The authors will nevertheless verify the specific conference venue's ethics,
data-use, and disclosure requirements before submission.
